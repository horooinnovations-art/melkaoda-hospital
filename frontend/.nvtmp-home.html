Services· 06

What we offer

Core clinical services running across outpatient, inpatient, and emergency care — every one staffed by a dedicated team.

View all services
  1. I

    General Outpatient Care Services

    General Outpatient Care Services are medical services provided to patients who visit the hospital for diagnosis, treatment, or follow-up without being admitted to the hospital.

    Explore unit
  2. II

    Pediatric Consultation & Child Care

    This service focuses on the medical care of infants, children, and adolescents, including routine checkups, illness diagnosis, growth monitoring, immunization guidance, and management of childhood diseases.

    Explore unit
  3. III

    Emergency and Critical Care

    This service delivers immediate medical care for life-threatening conditions, trauma, accidents, and acute illnesses. Available 24/7, the emergency team ensures rapid assessment, stabilization, and referral or admission as required.

    Explore unit
  4. IV

    Laboratory Services

    Diagnostic Laboratory Services are hospital services that perform scientific testing of blood, body fluids, tissues, and other samples to help detect, diagnose, monitor, and prevent diseases.

    Explore unit
  5. V

    Imaging / Radiology Services

    Imaging (Radiology) Services are hospital services that use medical imaging technologies to visualize internal organs and structures for accurate diagnosis, treatment planning, and disease monitoring.

    Explore unit
  6. VI

    Inpatient Care Services (Medical and Surgical)

    Inpatient Care Services (Medical and Surgical) are hospital services provided to patients who are admitted and stay in the hospital for continuous medical observation, treatment, and recovery.

    Explore unit
